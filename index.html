Frequency-Modulated Piezoelectric Haptic Display

MSC Lab, University of California, Berkeley
In Submission

Too-Long-Didn't-Read Abstract

Motivation: Existing haptic displays usually has small pixels but bulky on-skin driver circuits, barring them from wearability and scalability.

Contribution: Proposed a new design where the on-skin driver system is compact, revealing a path towards wearable large-scale haptic glove.

Approach: Haptic pixels use higher frequency instead of larger deformation to represent stronger haptic sense. All pixels share the same power sinusoidal wave source.
